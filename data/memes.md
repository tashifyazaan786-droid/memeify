## Gucci Morty

**Aliases:** Gucci Rick, Morty Gucci, drip Morty
**Verified:** Yes (curated seed entry)
**Sources:** Hackathon team curation
**Era:** 2024–2025
**Origin:** An AI-generated image trend depicting Rick and Morty characters
(most often Morty) dressed in Gucci/designer streetwear with exaggerated
"drip" — chains, monogram fabric, sunglasses — created using image
generation tools and spread heavily on Instagram Reels and TikTok.
**Meaning:** Used to mock or celebrate someone dressing above their
apparent status, or simply as absurdist AI-art humor. Part of a wider wave
of "AI brainrot" content mixing cartoon characters with luxury fashion.
**Cultural Context:** Emerged alongside a broader trend of AI image/video
generators being used to remix cartoon IP into surreal, oddly-detailed
scenes, which itself became a meme format ("AI slop" aesthetic used
ironically).

## IShowSpeed GTA (Real GTA 6 Reaction)

**Verified:** Yes (curated seed entry)
**Sources:** Hackathon team curation
**Era:** 2025

## Parent Split-Face Format

**Aliases:** Mom/Dad combined face, genetic lottery meme
**Verified:** Yes (curated seed entry)
**Sources:** Hackathon team curation
**Era:** 2023–2025
**Origin:** A TikTok/Instagram trend where a creator splits their face
down the middle and morphs one half toward their mother's face and the
other half toward their father's face, usually set to a specific trending
audio, to show which parent they resemble more.
**Meaning:** Playful format for family content, resemblance jokes, and
"genetic lottery" commentary (winning or losing it based on which side
looks better).
**Cultural Context:** Part of a broader family of face-morph filters/trends
using TikTok's face-tracking effects.

## GG EZ

**Aliases:** GGEZ, "good game easy"
**Verified:** Yes (curated seed entry)
**Sources:** Hackathon team curation
**Era:** 2010s–present (evergreen gaming meme)
**Origin:** Originated in competitive online gaming (Counter-Strike,
League of Legends, Dota) as a taunt typed in chat after a lopsided win —
"gg" (good game) followed by "ez" (easy) to imply the opponent barely
posed a challenge.
**Meaning:** Used both literally in-game as trash talk and, more broadly
online, sarcastically or genuinely to describe any effortless win or
victory in a non-gaming context (an exam, an argument, a sports match).
**Cultural Context:** One of the oldest and most evergreen gaming-chat
memes; still widely used as a general "that was easy" phrase.

**Verified:** Yes (curated seed entry)
**Sources:** Hackathon team curation

**Origin:** Clips of Bhojpuri actor and MP Ravi Kishan speaking in a mix

## Aura Farming

**Aliases:** Farming aura, aura points, "negative aura"
**Verified:** Yes (curated seed entry)
**Sources:** Hackathon team curation
**Era:** 2024–2025
**Origin:** Grew out of Gen Alpha/Z slang where "aura" describes a
person's perceived coolness or charisma. "Aura farming" describes
deliberately doing something stylish, smooth, or effortlessly cool
specifically to gain "aura points" — often used about a viral clip of a
boy calmly steering a boat with a confident expression, which became the
poster image for the phrase.
**Meaning:** Can be used sincerely (that was a cool, aura-gaining moment)
or sarcastically (mocking someone trying too hard to look cool, "losing
aura" from a cringe or awkward moment).
**Cultural Context:** Part of the broader Gen Alpha slang wave alongside
"rizz," "gyatt," and "skibidi," largely spread through YouTube Shorts,
TikTok, and gaming livestreams.

## iPhone Launch Queue Memes (India)

**Aliases:** iPhone line jokes, Apple store queue memes, "flex over
function"
**Verified:** Yes (curated seed entry)
**Sources:** Hackathon team curation
**Era:** Recurring every September (India)
**Origin:** Every new iPhone launch, Indian social media fills with
photos/videos of long queues outside Apple stores (especially in Mumbai

slots, or taking loans/EMIs to afford the phone.

mockery of buying an expensive phone on installments.
**Cultural Context:** An annually recurring Indian meme cycle tied
directly to Apple's launch calendar; often crosses over with jokes about
salary and EMI culture (see "Salary Day / EMI Memes").

## Salary Day / EMI Memes (India)

**Aliases:** Salary credited meme, EMI se pehle, "1st ko amir, 28th ko
fakir"
**Verified:** Yes (curated seed entry)
**Sources:** Hackathon team curation
**Era:** Evergreen (India)
**Origin:** A long-running genre of Indian meme content around the
monthly cycle of salary credit, immediately followed by rent, EMI
(installment) payments, and UPI spending wiping out the balance within
days.
**Meaning:** Self-deprecating humor about the gap between salary day
excitement and being broke again almost immediately once EMIs, rent, and
UPI spends are deducted.
**Cultural Context:** Closely tied to India's UPI-driven spending habits
and the popularity of EMI/"buy now pay later" schemes among young
earners; a staple of Indian meme pages and finance-adjacent content
creators.

## UPI Payment Memes (India)

**Aliases:** "Scan kar do," UPI down meme, ₹1 UPI test
**Verified:** Yes (curated seed entry)
**Sources:** Hackathon team curation
**Era:** Evergreen (India)
**Origin:** Memes built around India's near-universal UPI (Unified
Payments Interface) digital payment culture — jokes about paying a
street vendor via QR code, sending exactly ₹1 to test if UPI is working,
or panic when UPI servers go down nationally.
**Meaning:** Reflects how central UPI has become to daily Indian life,
to the point that even small vendors expect digital payment; "UPI down"
memes specifically capture mass anxiety when the payment rails fail.
**Cultural Context:** A uniquely Indian meme category, since UPI adoption
is far higher in India than equivalent instant-payment systems elsewhere.

## Cooked

**Aliases:** "He's cooked," "bro thought he cooked," "we're so cooked"
**Verified:** Yes (curated seed entry)
**Sources:** Hackathon team curation
**Era:** 2023–2025
**Origin:** Slang meaning "in serious trouble" or "finished" (from the
idea of being "done for" like food that's finished cooking), popularized

making a fatal in-game mistake.

impressive but actually embarrassed himself.
**Cultural Context:** Part of the same Gen Alpha/Z slang wave as "aura,"
"rizz," and "locked in," spread mainly through gaming clips and reaction
TikToks.

## Brainrot (General Term)

**Aliases:** "That's brainrot," brainrot content
**Verified:** Yes (curated seed entry)
**Sources:** Hackathon team curation
**Era:** 2023–2025
**Origin:** Originally a term for low-quality, addictive short-form
content consumed in excess (often aimed at or associated with young
children/teens), later adopted self-referentially by the same audiences
consuming it, including AI-generated absurdist content (Skibidi Toilet,
Italian brainrot animals, Gucci Morty-style AI images).
**Meaning:** Can be critical ("this content is rotting your brain") or
self-aware/affectionate (posting something absurd and captioning it
"brainrot" ironically).
**Cultural Context:** Oxford's 2024 Word of the Year; widely used both
as generational critique and generational in-joke, straddling the line
between insult and identity marker for younger internet users.

**Aliases:** so over, so back, over/back cycle
**Verified:** Yes (curated seed entry)
**Sources:** Hackathon team curation
**Era:** 2020–present

## "No Cap" / Cap

**Aliases:** cap, no cap, capping
**Verified:** Yes (curated seed entry)
**Sources:** Hackathon team curation
**Era:** mid-2010s–present (AAVE → mainstream)
**Origin:** African American Vernacular English; "cap" means a lie or exaggeration, "no cap" means "no lie / for real." Spread via hip-hop, Vine, and later TikTok into global Gen Z slang.
